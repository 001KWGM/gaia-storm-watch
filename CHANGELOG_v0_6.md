- Updated visual styling to better align with the GAIA Marine website direction: darker marine hero panel, restrained GAIA gold accent, softer cards, Inter/Montserrat typography, and cleaner public-story layout.
- Added source/time captions below key maps, graphs, tables, camera cards, weather-reference cards and public digest content.

- Strengthened footer copyright/source wording: no third-party camera/radar/weather-map/satellite images are copied, downloaded, archived or rehosted by GAIA Marine in this prototype.

- Updated registration acknowledgement wording to include commercial decision-making limitations.
- Added stronger source-register caveats for commercial reliance and operational decision-making.
- Aligned contact email to the GAIA Marine website direction: solutions@gaia-marine.ai.

## Public-user boundary

Public users can select and view curated content only. They cannot upload data, change thresholds, trigger processing, change source selection or alter internal settings.
